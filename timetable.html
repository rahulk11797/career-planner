🧠 Implementation Intentions

Research: "If-then" planning makes follow-through 2-3x more likely (Gollwitzer, 1999)

IF it's 8:00 AM →

Open LeetCode and solve DSA problems

IF it's 8:00 PM →

Open IDE and work on [yesterday's planned task]

IF I feel unmotivated →

Do only 2 minutes of review (see Minimum Viable Day)

IF it's 9:30 PM →

Write tomorrow's exact topic + intent in one sentence

🔥

Minimum Viable Day (2-Minute Rule)

Research: Starting is 90% of habit formation. Never do zero. (BJ Fogg, Stanford)

10 min

Review yesterday's notes

1 problem

Solve any easy DSA

5 min

Read one concept page

→ A bad day with 10 min still counts. The streak doesn't break.

⚡ Energy-Based Scheduling

Research: Match task difficulty to energy level, not clock time (Schwartz, The Power of Full Engagement)

New concepts, hard problems, deep learning

8:00 – 9:00 AM block

🟡 MEDIUM ENERGY (EVENING)

8:00 – 9:30 PM block

🟢 LOW ENERGY (COMMUTE)

Drive time blocks

🔀 Interleaving Practice

Research: Mixing topics = 43% better retention (Rohrer & Taylor, 2007)

Evening block: Don't do only DSA. Mix in 1 system design question or 1 Java concept.

Example: 2 DSA problems → 1 system design sketch → 1 Spring concept

Why it works: Feels harder in the moment, but builds stronger connections.

🔁 Spaced Repetition

Research: You forget 70% in 24h without review (Ebbinghaus, 1885)

Day 1: Learn new concept

Day 2: Quick recall during morning review

Day 5 (Fri): Recall all week's topics without notes

Day 7 (Sun): Weekly review — mark what stuck
